THE LINEUP

Seven distinct musical forces uniting to forge the Alpha Rhythms sound. Est. 2021.

01 Shawn
FOUNDER • DRUMMER #01 / 07

Shawn

Founder and drummer of Alpha Rhythms. Shawn sets the pace with powerful grooves and rock-solid beats that drive the whole band.

"We play with pure energy from the first beat to the last."
02 Keerthan
LEAD MALE VOCALIST #02 / 07

Keerthan

Lead male vocalist and frontman. Keerthan brings big energy, high notes, and a soulful voice that gets every crowd singing along.

"Every song is a chance to connect with the audience."
03 Vinisha
LEAD FEMALE VOCALIST #03 / 07

Vinisha

Lead female vocalist. Vinisha delivers rich melodies, beautiful harmonies, and strong vocals that light up every live show.

"Music brings people together, and that's why we perform."
04 Hans
LEAD GUITARIST #04 / 07

Hans

Lead guitarist. Hans plays catchy riffs and electric guitar solos that give Alpha Rhythms its rock sound.

"Plug in, turn it up, and let the music speak."
05 Joyston
BASS GUITARIST #05 / 07

Joyston

Bass guitarist. Joyston brings the deep rhythm and heavy basslines that keep the dance floor moving.

"When the bass hits right, the whole room feels it."
06 Ashton
KEYBOARDIST & SYNTH #06 / 07

Ashton

Keyboardist and synth player. Ashton adds smooth piano chords, catchy melodies, and rich sounds to every track.

"Good melodies stay in your head long after the concert."
07 Kenneth
PERCUSSIONIST #07 / 07

Kenneth

Percussionist. Kenneth adds energetic rhythms and lively beats that make every concert a celebration.

"Rhythm is what turns a good song into a dance party."
